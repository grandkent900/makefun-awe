A five-line poem that gives the AI judge a slow traveller to follow: starlight that left its star a thousand years ago, still on its way, arriving at a small window to rest. Nothing in it orders the reader to relax; the calm comes from the image itself — a journey measured in centuries, ending in a darkness wide enough, and unhurried enough, to hold it. The program writes the poem (235 characters, 239 including line breaks) to `entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
